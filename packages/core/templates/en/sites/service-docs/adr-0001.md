## Context

We need to record the architecture decisions of this service: what was
decided, why, and what we accepted with it. Without a record, whoever comes
later does not know whether a choice can change or what it costs.

## Decision

Every important decision becomes an ADR (architecture decision record): a
short page in this folder, numbered in the order the decision was made.

## Consequences

- Every ADR has a status, context, decision, consequences and alternatives
- A revisited decision becomes a new ADR that points to the old one
- The next ADR comes from the "New page from template…" command

## Alternatives considered

- Decisions only in chats and meetings: they fade over time
- A single architecture document: it mixes the current state with history
