# The TechDocs sidebar

The book icon in the Activity Bar shows up in workspaces with a `mkdocs.yml`.

| Section | What it shows |
|---------|---------------|
| **Documentation** | The pages in the order of the site menu (the `nav`), with their titles. Pages left out of the menu are flagged. |
| **Check** | What TechDocs needs and is missing or wrong, grouped by kind, each with a fix when there is one. |
| **Publishing** | Draft, check and send for review, when the panel is turned on. |
